# Frequently asked questions

## Where should I ask?

Open an issue in the affected repository's Issues tab for bugs, feature requests, documentation corrections and device reports. Use [discussions](https://github.com/orgs/pauhu/discussions) for questions, ideas and sharing your work. Issues and discussions are public; do not paste keys or personal data.

For sales, partnerships or procurement, [contact us privately](https://pauhu.ai/contact/). For security vulnerabilities, use [private reporting](https://github.com/pauhu/.github/security/policy), not a public issue.
