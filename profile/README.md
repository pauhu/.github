# Pauhu

Pauhu is a modular AI system. Each repository below is one part you can plug into your own tools, with sourced answers and a receipt.

## The Pauhu Menu

Every Pauhu repository, in one place. Each one has the same quickstart and works with the same developer key.

---

## TOOLS

*Parts you plug into your own tools*

**[Pauhu Client](https://github.com/pauhu/ai-assistant)**  
Ask Pauhu sourced questions from your terminal.

**[Pauhu Measurement Provenance](https://github.com/pauhu/data-provenance)**  
Client for reading a local measurement record with its origin; checks come from the Pauhu demo backend.

**[Pauhu Edge AI](https://github.com/pauhu/edge-ai)**  
Pauhu senses (reading, hearing, speaking, seeing) on your own device; the model pack is served by the Pauhu demo backend.

**[Pauhu Bench](https://github.com/pauhu/llm-benchmark)**  
Client for Pauhu small-model benchmarks served by the Pauhu demo backend.

**[Pauhu Device Firewall](https://github.com/pauhu/llm-security)**  
Firewall and network setup for devices that call the Pauhu AI API.

**[Pauhu Folder Assistant](https://github.com/pauhu/local-ai)**  
Client for running a Pauhu plan over a folder you granted; the plan comes from the Pauhu demo backend.

**[Pauhu MCP Server](https://github.com/pauhu/mcp-server)**  
Connect your AI assistant to Pauhu over MCP.

**[Pauhu Linux Client](https://github.com/pauhu/pauhu-linux)**  
Pauhu command line for Linux.

**[Pauhu Post-Quantum Cryptography](https://github.com/pauhu/post-quantum-cryptography)**  
Proposed: post-quantum key encapsulation and signatures through an open library.

**[Pauhu Database Client](https://github.com/pauhu/database-client)**  
Client for reading records from a granted local database without modifying it; plans and receipts come from the Pauhu demo backend.

**[Pauhu Tool Calling](https://github.com/pauhu/tool-calling)**  
Tool calling for AI models: your model calls tools that run on your device, following tool plans from the Pauhu demo backend.

**[Pauhu Web Search](https://github.com/pauhu/web-search)**  
Web answers that cite the page they came from, with its version date where the publisher gives one and the retrieval time.

## METHODS

*Open methods for building with AI agents*

**[Pauhu Coding Agent](https://github.com/pauhu/coding-agent)**  
Develop software for regulated environments with a coding agent, with traceable evidence.

**[Pauhu Spec-Driven Development](https://github.com/pauhu/spec-driven-development)**  
Numbered specs, checkable criteria, tests that name them.

**[Pauhu Context Engineering](https://github.com/pauhu/context-engineering)**  
Give AI models the right sourced context, and measure it.

**[Pauhu Multi-Agent Work](https://github.com/pauhu/multi-agent)**  
Run several AI agents on shared work with coordinated claims, reviews and receipts.

**[Pauhu AI Governance](https://github.com/pauhu/ai-governance)**  
Register, assess, change and monitor AI systems with evidence.

**[Pauhu Forecasting](https://github.com/pauhu/forecasting)**  
Write checkable forecasts, score them against outcomes and baselines.


---

## METHODS FOR MODELS AND MACHINES

*Open methods for models, data and devices*

**[Pauhu Quantization](https://github.com/pauhu/quantization)**  
Shrink a model and measure what it loses on a held-out set.

**[Pauhu Fine-Tuning](https://github.com/pauhu/fine-tuning)**  
Adapt a small model to your own sourced data, with held-out tests.

**[Pauhu Knowledge Graph](https://github.com/pauhu/knowledge-graph)**  
A read-only graph over your databases, every fact traced to its row.

**[Pauhu Physical AI](https://github.com/pauhu/physical-ai)**  
Civilian robot risk assessment and pre-action safety checks; checks alone do not make a robot safe.

**[Pauhu CAN Bus Logging](https://github.com/pauhu/can-bus)**  
Read-only vehicle and machine data logs on your device, OBD2 included.

**[Pauhu Medical Devices](https://github.com/pauhu/medical-devices)**  
Read-only analysis of device logs kept local, with privacy and safety review checks; no diagnosis or alarm handling.
