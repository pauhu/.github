# Contributing

Issues and discussions are public; do not paste keys or personal data.

## Where to go

Open an issue in the affected repository's Issues tab for bugs, feature requests, documentation corrections and device reports. Use [discussions](https://github.com/orgs/pauhu/discussions) for questions, ideas and sharing your work. For sales, partnerships or procurement, [contact us privately](https://pauhu.ai/contact/). Report security vulnerabilities through [private reporting](https://github.com/pauhu/.github/security/policy), not a public issue.

## Ownership and terms

Pauhu Ltd owns the repositories and contribution terms. Pauhu AI Ltd operates pauhu.ai and its keys and serves its customers. Check each repository's licence and contribution terms before contributing.
